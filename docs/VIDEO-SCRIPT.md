## Sources for each claim
- "more than twice the old rule": 0.862 vs 0.391 held-out CinC 2015 (`docs/evidence/ML-RESULTS.md`). "eighty-five percent" / "fewer than three in a hundred": 85.1% (97/114) and 2.7% (2/74) at the live thresholds (`docs/evidence/raw/fatigue.txt`).
- 12-bed ward replaying PhysioNet/CinC 2015, VTaC, MIMIC-III waveforms, VitalDB (`README.md` sources). Ward times: final runs F1-F3 and night run N (`docs/evidence/WARD-RUN.md`).
- Six containers (`docs/evidence/CONTAINERS.md`), 15/15 security tests, TLS 1.3, per-monitor accounts (`docs/evidence/SECURITY.md`), 96 beds per brain (`docs/evidence/SCALE.md`), AI-chip second opinion shown only (`docs/evidence/ML-RESULTS.md`).

## Honesty notes
- A lab ward replaying real recordings; never claim a hospital deployment.
- Alarm-fatigue numbers are advice only; never say alarms are cut or silenced.
- The on-chip AI is a shown-only second opinion and is not faster than a CPU core at this model size; do not claim speed.
